# How to use Nomily AI

Nomily is your AI recorder: it turns speech into structured notes, and everything stays on your own phone.

## Three steps
- Record: with a recorder card connected, tap record on the home page — or record straight from the iPhone, or import a file
- Transcribe: a finished recording transcribes itself, with speakers separated
- Organise: AI writes the summary, action items, outline and mind map; the Source tab jumps back to the exact words
