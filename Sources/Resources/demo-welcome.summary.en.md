# Welcome to Nomily AI

Hello, and thanks for downloading Nomily AI — an app that turns recordings into notes you can actually use.

## What this library is
The Library you are looking at holds all of your recordings and notes. So you can get a feel for it, we put three items in it:
- "How to use Nomily AI": a three-step guide — read this one first
- "Product Weekly (sample)": a real sample recording. Open it to listen, and to see the AI summary, action items, mind map, source, outline and sentence-by-sentence transcript
- This welcome note

## What you can do
- Connect a Nomily recorder card and let recordings sync themselves
- Record directly from the iPhone or Apple Watch
- Let AI write summaries, action items and mind maps
- Back up to your own NAS or iCloud

## Our privacy promise
Your recordings and notes stay on your device by default. Cloud transcription only starts once you configure a provider yourself — nothing passes through Nomily's servers.

Enjoy!
